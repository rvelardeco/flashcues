Questions? Answers.

What does “beta” mean?

FlashCues is ready to use, but still being polished. Expect improvements and the odd rough edge, and please tell us what you find.

Why does macOS ask me to confirm the first time?

The beta isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm you trust it. It’s a one-time step that takes about 30 seconds, and the “Read Me First” file in the download walks you through it. The Mac App Store version won’t need it.

Will it be on the Mac App Store?

Yes, it’s coming soon.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the speech model (about 700 MB) after you agree. After that it works completely offline.

What audio can it listen to?

Any input your Mac can see: a USB audio interface, a comms belt-pack feed, or the built-in mic.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Audio is processed on your Mac and transcripts are saved only on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
