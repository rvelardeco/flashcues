Questions? Answers.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. FlashCues is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, which means Apple checked it for malicious software. It opens like any other Mac app. There’s no trip to System Settings › Privacy & Security and nothing to override.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and FlashCues is yours. No account, no monthly fee.

Will it be on the Mac App Store?

Yes, it’s coming soon.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the speech model (about 700 MB) after you agree. After that it works completely offline.

What audio can it listen to?

Any input your Mac can see: a USB audio interface, a comms belt-pack feed, or the built-in mic.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Audio is processed on your Mac and transcripts are saved only on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
