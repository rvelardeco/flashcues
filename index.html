FAQ

What does "beta" mean?

FlashCues works today and is used in real shows, but it's still being polished. Expect updates and the odd rough edge, and tell us what you find.

Will it be on the Mac App Store?

Yes, it's coming soon to the Mac App Store.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the speech model (about 700 MB) after you agree. After that it transcribes fully offline.

What audio can it listen to?

Any input your Mac sees: a USB audio interface, a comms belt-pack feed, or the built-in mic.

Is my audio recorded or uploaded?

No. Audio and transcripts stay on your Mac. See the privacy policy.

What do I need?

A Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
